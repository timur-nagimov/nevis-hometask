# ds-rvlcdip-types

Автор: Claude subagent (dataset builder; labels from RVL-CDIP classes, a written mapping and string checks), 2026-11-20.

Document-type search over real scanned office pages. Dataset: RVL-CDIP with Amazon Textract OCR, Hugging Face stefan-hf/rvlcdip-redact (https://huggingface.co/datasets/stefan-hf/rvlcdip-redact), config textract_redact, split test, revision a1f0f0406f9c146340713f03f06898ccfff4c073. Licence: other: the dataset card applies the terms of the Legacy Tobacco Document Library / IIT-CDIP, https://www.industrydocuments.ucsf.edu/help/copyright/. Attribution: OCR text of RVL-CDIP test pages (A. W. Harley, A. Ufkes, K. G. Derpanis, ICDAR 2015), a subset of IIT-CDIP (D. Lewis et al., SIGIR 2006) drawn from the Legacy Tobacco Document Library, UCSF Industry Documents Library; Amazon Textract OCR and corrected labels from stefan-hf/rvlcdip-redact (S. Larson et al., DocEng 2025). Corpus: 195 pages, 13 per class for 15 of the 16 classes, sampled with seed 20261120 from 800 rows in seeded 100-row blocks of the test split; a page qualifies when its original and corrected labels agree, its OCR text has at least 30 words and at most 5000 characters. 'file folder' is left out: none of its pages in the sample has 30 words. Content is the OCR text as is (noisy); titles are neutral ('Scan 0001'); pages are spread over 5 placeholder clients. Labels: class queries take relevance from the RVL-CDIP labels; purpose queries from this written mapping: 'someone's work history' -> resume; 'price list or bill' -> invoice + budget; 'planned spending' -> budget; 'internal office note' -> memo; 'promotional material for a product' -> advertisement; 'technical requirements for a product' -> specification; 'press coverage' -> news article; 'research findings' -> scientific report + scientific publication; acceptable pages and negative queries come from a whole-word string check of the query words (fixed -s/-es/-ed/-d/-ing forms); detail strings occur in exactly one page. Built by research/datasets/build/ds_rvlcdip_types.py from research/datasets/raw/rvlcdip/. Pages per class: advertisement 13, budget 13, email 13, form 13, handwritten 13, invoice 13, letter 13, memo 13, news article 13, presentation 13, questionnaire 13, resume 13, scientific publication 13, scientific report 13, specification 13.

Корпус: 5 клиентов, 195 документов (полный текст — в `suites/ds-rvlcdip-types.json`).
