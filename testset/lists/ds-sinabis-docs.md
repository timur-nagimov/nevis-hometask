# ds-sinabis-docs

Автор: Claude subagent (dataset builder, blind to research/_design), 2026-11-20.

Documents from the open dataset sinabis-1m-synthetic-pdf (https://huggingface.co/datasets/sinabis-group/sinabis-1m-synthetic-pdf, revision 92c46bb9a008cfb77cbfc9382fbf2ecfd434ded8, licence CC BY 4.0). Attribution: 'Sinabis Green Energy - Synthetic Document Dataset (1M PDFs)' by sinabis-group, CC BY 4.0; documents sampled, nothing else changed. Every person, company, address, phone number and account in it is fictional (.example domains, 555 numbers). The dataset has no UK issuer, so the sample uses English documents from the US, Australia and New Zealand. Corpus: 166 documents with a text layer under 5,000 characters, addressed to a private person, sampled with a fixed seed from shard train-00000: authority_notice 8 (AU 1, NZ 2, US 5); bank_statement 20 (AU 6, NZ 7, US 7); credit_note 8 (AU 1, US 7); dunning 14 (AU 4, US 10); hotel_invoice 8 (AU 2, US 6); invoice 16 (AU 3, US 13); order_confirmation 6 (US 6); payslip 20 (AU 6, NZ 5, US 9); quote 8 (AU 2, US 6); receipt 10 (AU 1, NZ 2, US 7); utility_bill 40 (AU 7, NZ 5, US 28); workshop_invoice 8 (AU 1, NZ 1, US 6). Titles are the dataset's own file names; content is the dataset's extracted text. The 8 clients are payslip holders from the dataset; documents are attached to them at random, so client names are not tested here (scope documents). Build script: research/datasets/build/ds_sinabis_docs.py; raw rows: research/datasets/raw/sinabis/. Purpose-to-type mapping. Proof of address. (1) utility_bill: relevant. JSA (https://www.gov.uk/jobseekers-allowance/your-jsa-interview): 'Utility bills can be used for proof of address and as further proof of identity if they are from different suppliers'; its proof-of-address list: 'utility bill dated within the last 6 months'. Bona Vacantia (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/proof-of-identity-checklist/proof-of-identity-checklist): 'Utility bill (gas, electric, satellite television, landline phone bill) issued within the last three months'. Bona Vacantia also says 'Documents we will not accept include ... Mobile phone bills', so utility bills mentioning 'mobile' were left out of the sample. (2) bank_statement: relevant. Bona Vacantia: 'Bank, Building Society or Credit Union statement or passbook dated within the last three months'. (3) payslip: acceptable: named only on the JSA list ('payslip or pension statement dated within the last 6 months'), not on the Bona Vacantia list (edited by the lead on 2026-11-20; the first build had it relevant). (4) authority_notice: acceptable: not on either list (they name 'Council Tax bill', 'HMRC self-assessment letters or tax demand', 'original notification letter from Benefits Agency'), but an official letter to the holder's address is commonly accepted elsewhere. (5) invoice, hotel_invoice, workshop_invoice, dunning, receipt, quote, credit_note, order_confirmation: wrong (decoys). Further proof of identity: utility_bill relevant (JSA quote above); bank_statement acceptable (JSA: 'current debit, credit or store card with a statement confirming the card details'); payslip and authority_notice acceptable; decoys wrong. Proof of income: neither list covers income, so the mapping uses GOV.UK Family visas, Proof of your finances (https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/provide-information): 'If you or your family member are employed, you could include: bank statements showing your or their income; 6 months of payslips ...' and 'income from employment before tax and National Insurance (from P60 or payslips)'. payslip: relevant; bank_statement: relevant when its text shows a salary, net-pay, wages, payroll or pension credit (all sampled ones do), otherwise acceptable; everything else wrong. Literal and short queries follow the theme labels; a document of another type whose own text contains the query phrase is acceptable. Utility sub-types (electricity, water, gas, broadband) are not labelled in the dataset and are taken from each bill's own text. Detail queries take a document number or the issuer and total from the target document. Negative queries are checked by the script: none of their key terms occurs in the corpus.

Корпус: 8 клиентов, 166 документов (полный текст — в `suites/ds-sinabis-docs.json`).
