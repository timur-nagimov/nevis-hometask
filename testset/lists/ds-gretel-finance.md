# ds-gretel-finance

Автор: Claude subagent (dataset builder, labels from document types and GOV.UK lists; did not read any search design), 2026-11-20.

Document-search suite built from gretelai/synthetic_pii_finance_multilingual (https://huggingface.co/datasets/gretelai/synthetic_pii_finance_multilingual), revision 7b844d16738527a04264f50214cb426a4cea0897, file data/English_train-00000-of-00001.parquet, licence Apache-2.0. Attribution: documents from gretelai/synthetic_pii_finance_multilingual by Gretel.ai, Apache-2.0; synthetic text generated with Gretel Navigator; sampled and not modified. Every name, address and number in the documents is synthetic. Official lists: https://www.gov.uk/jobseekers-allowance/your-jsa-interview and https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/proof-of-identity-checklist/proof-of-identity-checklist (both checked 2026-11-20, Open Government Licence v3.0).

Corpus: 20 English document types x 8 documents = 160, sampled with seed 'ds-gretel-finance-v1' (rows ordered by sha256 of seed and row id) from rows of 300-5000 characters; longer rows were skipped, never cut, and rows opening with the generator's chatter ('Sure, here is ...') were skipped. Only subtypes (`expanded_type`) that really are the labelled kind of document were kept (the list is in research/datasets/build/ds_gretel_finance.py); student-loan subtypes were left out because the JSA page counts student loan documentation as proof of address. Title = the document's own first text line when it is a heading, otherwise 'Document NNNN'; no type label was added. Content = the full generated text. 10 clients are account holders printed in bank and card statements; a document goes to the client whose name it prints, the rest are spread round-robin. Build: research/datasets/build/ds_gretel_finance.py; raw rows: research/datasets/raw/gretel/.

Labels come only from the dataset's `document_type` plus the mappings below, or (detail queries) from the target document's own text. Text rule for literal and category queries: a document of another type whose own text contains the searched term is acceptable, never required. Purpose queries have no text rule. Every non-empty type, category and purpose query also requires a relevant document first. Some queries have up to 40 relevant documents, so a search that caps its results below that cannot pass them.

Literal type queries (query -> relevant types; acceptable types):
- 'bank statement' -> Bank Statement; acceptable: Credit Card Statement. Literal type. Credit card statements are account statements too: a near miss, acceptable.
- 'credit card statement' -> Credit Card Statement; acceptable: Bank Statement. Literal type. Bank statements are account statements too: a near miss, acceptable.
- 'tax return' -> Tax Return; acceptable: Tax Assessment Notice. Literal type. Tax assessment notices belong to the same tax cycle: acceptable.
- 'tax assessment notice' -> Tax Assessment Notice; acceptable: Tax Return. Literal type. Tax returns belong to the same tax cycle: acceptable.
- 'pension plan' -> Pension Plan Agreement; acceptable: none. Literal type in an adviser's words; the documents are pension plan agreements.
- 'employment contract' -> Employment Contract; acceptable: none. Literal type (the dataset's Employment Contract, including internship and probationary contracts).
- 'mortgage agreement' -> Mortgage Contract; acceptable: Mortgage Amortization Schedule. The documents say 'mortgage contract'. Amortization schedules of a mortgage are acceptable.
- 'amortisation schedule' -> Mortgage Amortization Schedule; acceptable: Mortgage Contract. UK spelling; the documents say 'amortization'. Mortgage contracts are acceptable.
- 'loan agreement' -> Loan Agreement; acceptable: Mortgage Contract. Literal type. A mortgage contract is a loan agreement secured on a property: acceptable.
- 'loan application' -> Loan Application; acceptable: Credit Application. Literal type. Credit applications ask for a loan or credit line too: acceptable.
- 'credit application' -> Credit Application; acceptable: Loan Application. Literal type. Loan applications are applications for credit too: acceptable.
- 'insurance policy' -> Insurance Policy; acceptable: Renewal Reminder, Insurance Claim Form. Literal type. Renewal reminders and claim forms refer to a policy: acceptable.
- 'insurance claim form' -> Insurance Claim Form; acceptable: none. Literal type.
- 'insurance renewal notice' -> Renewal Reminder; acceptable: Insurance Policy. UK wording for the dataset's Renewal Reminder. The policies themselves are acceptable.
- 'financial statements' -> Financial Statement; acceptable: Financial Disclosure Statement. Literal type (company accounts). A personal financial disclosure statement is a near miss: acceptable.
- 'fund prospectus' -> Investment Prospectus; acceptable: Securities Prospectus, Product Disclosure Statement. Investment prospectuses describe funds. Bond prospectuses and product disclosures are near misses.
- 'bond prospectus' -> Securities Prospectus; acceptable: Investment Prospectus. Every sampled securities prospectus offers bonds or notes. Fund prospectuses are near misses.
- 'trade confirmation' -> Trade Confirmation; acceptable: none. Literal type.
- 'contract note' -> Trade Confirmation; acceptable: none. UK term for a trade confirmation; no document uses it.
- 'investment portfolio statement' -> Financial Disclosure Statement; acceptable: none. The sampled financial disclosure statements are all personal investment portfolio statements.
- 'product disclosure statement' -> Product Disclosure Statement; acceptable: none. Literal type. A financial disclosure statement shares two words but is a different document.
- 'key features document' -> Product Disclosure Statement; acceptable: Investment Prospectus, Securities Prospectus. UK name of the pre-sale product disclosure; prospectuses are acceptable offer documents.

Category queries (query -> relevant types; acceptable types: reason):
- 'loan documents' -> Loan Agreement, Loan Application, Credit Application; acceptable: Mortgage Contract, Mortgage Amortization Schedule: Loan agreements and applications for a loan or credit are the loan file; mortgages are loans too but advisers file and search them as mortgages, so they only partly belong (acceptable).
- 'mortgage paperwork' -> Mortgage Contract, Mortgage Amortization Schedule; acceptable: none: The mortgage contract and its amortization schedule are the mortgage file.
- 'pension paperwork' -> Pension Plan Agreement; acceptable: Product Disclosure Statement: Pension plan agreements; product disclosure statements partly belong (some describe retirement products).
- 'insurance documents' -> Insurance Policy, Insurance Claim Form, Renewal Reminder; acceptable: none: Policies, claim forms and renewal reminders are all insurance paperwork.
- 'investment documents' -> Investment Prospectus, Securities Prospectus, Trade Confirmation, Financial Disclosure Statement, Product Disclosure Statement; acceptable: Financial Statement: Prospectuses, trade confirmations, portfolio statements and product disclosures are investment paperwork; company financial statements are read for investment decisions but are not the client's investments (acceptable).
- 'tax paperwork' -> Tax Return, Tax Assessment Notice; acceptable: none: Tax returns and tax assessment notices.
- 'employment evidence' -> Employment Contract; acceptable: none: The employment contract is the only evidence of employment among the sampled types.
- 'debts and liabilities' -> Loan Agreement, Mortgage Contract, Mortgage Amortization Schedule, Credit Card Statement; acceptable: Loan Application, Credit Application: Loans, mortgages and credit card balances are liabilities; applications for credit are not debts yet (acceptable).
- 'account statements' -> Bank Statement, Credit Card Statement; acceptable: Financial Disclosure Statement, Mortgage Amortization Schedule: Bank and card statements; portfolio statements and loan schedules are statements of an account in a broad sense (acceptable).

Purpose queries (proof of address and identity) through the GOV.UK lists:
- Proof of address -> relevant Bank Statement: Bona Vacantia "Bank, Building Society or Credit Union statement or passbook dated within the last three months".
- Acceptable Tax Assessment Notice and Tax Return: Bona Vacantia "HMRC self-assessment letters or tax demand dated within the current financial year"; our notices are tax demands from various (mostly non-UK) authorities and a return is not the HMRC letter, so they only partly match.
- Acceptable Mortgage Contract and Mortgage Amortization Schedule: Bona Vacantia "Original mortgage statement from a recognised lender issued for the last full year"; the same mortgage, but not the statement.
- Acceptable Pension Plan Agreement and Employment Contract: JSA "Proof of address. Examples include a: payslip or pension statement dated within the last 6 months; utility bill dated within the last 6 months; Council Tax bill dated within the last 6 months; student loan documentation"; the same pension or job, but not the pension statement or payslip.
- Must not Credit Card Statement: Bona Vacantia "Documents we will not accept include, but are not limited to: Provisional driving licence; Mobile phone bills; Credit card statements".
- Further proof of identity -> relevant Credit Card Statement; acceptable Bank Statement, Employment Contract: JSA "Further proof of identity. Examples include your: P60; savings account book; personal cheque book; current debit, credit or store card with a statement confirming the card details".
- Photo ID -> empty: JSA "Photographic proof of identity. Examples include your: current passport; driving licence; eVisa; certificate of naturalisation as a British citizen; permanent residence permit"; none of these is in the corpus.
- All other types are wrong for these queries (the lists do not name insurance, loan, investment or company documents).

Negative queries name kinds of paperwork that are absent from the corpus and that no document mentions (utility bill, P60, birth certificate, tenancy agreement, will, marriage certificate) or are off-domain. 'Passport', 'payslip' and 'driving licence' were not used as negatives: some documents print a passport or driver's licence number or mention payslips.

Levels: required = literal type names and the assignment's 'address proof' wording, identifiers and company names; expected = synonyms and UK terms, categories, paraphrased purposes, amount-and-date lookups and negatives; nice = loosely supported mappings (key features document, further proof of identity).
