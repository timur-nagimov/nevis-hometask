# ds-enron-emails

Автор: Claude subagent (Opus 5.5), 2026-11-20; generated by research/datasets/build/ds_enron_emails.py, labels from EnronQA links and string checks only.

Real correspondence: 250 Enron e-mails from 10 mailboxes of EnronQA (MichaelR207/enron_qa_0922, revision c0b3a9190fd970e83cfbe7d399a08860e43e221e, test split, data/test-00000-of-00001.parquet). Mailboxes with 100-1000 e-mails that have test questions were eligible; 10 were drawn with seed 'ds-enron-emails-v1' (sha256 order), then 25 e-mails per mailbox in the same seeded order, skipping e-mails longer than 5000 characters after cleaning and exact duplicates. Each owner is a client (name and address from the most frequent first.last@enron.com address in the mailbox's Sender/Recipients; the data has no roles, so description is empty) and owns the e-mails of the mailbox. Title = subject; content = body without the quoted reply history (everything below the first 'Original Message' / 'Forwarded by' / header block when the sender wrote at least 40 characters above it; a bare forward keeps the forwarded text) and without the Enron disclaimer, '-- ' signatures and webmail ads (no reply history: 137, no reply history, signature removed: 4, reply history removed: 109). A question is used only if every word of it and of its gold answer that occurs in the original e-mail is still in the cleaned one. Queries: 50 EnronQA questions as written and 50 keyword versions (the source e-mail must be in the first 5 documents; other results are ignored because they carry no label); 30 people queries (full name, surname, e-mail local part: the client, plus its own e-mails and any e-mail containing every query word, nothing else); 15 negative questions from two mailboxes outside the corpus (e-mails not sent to or by a client, questions that name no client) whose gold and alternate answers occur in no corpus e-mail and whose e-mail shares no subject and under 10% of its 8-word sequences with any corpus e-mail (empty result). EnronQA gold answers are full sentences, so the 'another e-mail contains the gold answer' check rarely fires. Rebuild: uv run --python 3.13 --with duckdb python research/datasets/build/ds_enron_emails.py. Licence: The EnronQA dataset card declares no licence. The underlying Enron e-mails were made public by FERC during its investigation and are distributed by CMU without a formal licence; the people are real Enron employees. Attribution: Questions, answers and e-mail text from EnronQA (Michael J. Ryan, Danmei Xu, Chris Nivera, Daniel Campos, 'EnronQA: Towards Personalized RAG over Private Documents', arXiv:2505.00263, 2025; Hugging Face dataset MichaelR207/enron_qa_0922, revision c0b3a9190fd970e83cfbe7d399a08860e43e221e), built on the Enron Email Dataset released by FERC and distributed by William W. Cohen, CMU.
