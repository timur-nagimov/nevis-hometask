# ds-fiqa

Автор: Claude subagent (dataset import: FiQA-2018 via BEIR; labels from the human qrels, keyword forms written by the agent), 2026-11-20.

FiQA-2018 (financial opinion question answering, task 2) from the BEIR benchmark: real personal-finance forum questions with human relevance judgments, imported as a general check of the semantic (by-meaning) side of document search in the finance domain. Source: BeIR/fiqa (https://huggingface.co/datasets/BeIR/fiqa) at revision 979c07a7cb5ccc6ca009792241fa1250b98055dd (corpus and queries parquet) and BeIR/fiqa-qrels (https://huggingface.co/datasets/BeIR/fiqa-qrels) at revision 252958f2d646e22cab6d0c72dd3f0d5de6d0655a (test.tsv). Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (licence tag on both Hugging Face dataset cards). Attribution: FiQA-2018 data by Maia, Handschuh, Freitas, Davis, McDermott, Zarrouk and Balahur (WWW'18 Open Challenge: Financial Opinion Mining and Question Answering, The Web Conference 2018 Companion), in the BEIR packaging by Thakur, Reimers, Rücklé, Srivastava and Gurevych (BEIR, NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks), Hugging Face BeIR/fiqa and BeIR/fiqa-qrels, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: subset sampled, document titles generated from the first sentence, placeholder clients added, keyword forms of some questions written; this adapted subset is shared under the same licence. Corpus: 800 passages = 188 judged relevant to the sampled questions + 306 hard distractors (each shares at least 3 distinct content words with a sampled question and is not judged relevant to it; ranked by IDF-weighted word overlap divided by the square root of the passage's word count and taken round-robin: 5 for most questions, none for the 6 questions that no passage shares 3 content words with) + 306 random passages; passages over 5,000 characters, empty ones and exact duplicates were skipped. One passage = one document: title = its first sentence (joined with the next one when under 20 characters, CSV quote marks removed) cut to about 80 characters, content = the full passage as in the source; documents are spread over 5 placeholder clients (Forum Archive 1-5, example.org). Queries: 70 test questions sampled with seed 2018 from the 640 whose judged passages are all usable (category fiqa-question, verbatim), plus 25 keyword forms of 25 of them (4-6 words taken from the question, written by the importing agent; category fiqa-keywords) with the same labels. Every label comes from the qrels; relevant = all judged-relevant passages (1 to 10 per query), others = ignore because FiQA judgments are incomplete. A query passes only if every judged passage is in the returned list, so query the API with its largest limit (50). The questions are longer and more conversational than an adviser's typical query (median 10 words, up to 19), and the forum answers are informal; fiqa-keywords is closer to how an adviser types. Level expected throughout: FiQA is a general semantic check, not a requirement of the assignment. Built by research/datasets/build/ds_fiqa.py from research/datasets/raw/fiqa/.
